Who We Are

More Than a Training Hub

The Gifted Generation CIC is a community interest company. We help unemployed young adults aged 18 to 30 gain real, practical skills through hands-on vocational training. Alongside our courses we tackle two problems that hold young people and our community back: food insecurity and social isolation.

Vocational Training

Courses in painting & decorating, car bodywork, culinary arts and creative music.

Open Kitchen

Breakfast and dinner twice a week to combat food insecurity.

Gaming Hub

A welcoming social space to combat isolation and build friendships.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply the paint, or do I purchase it?

We can do either. We have trade accounts with Farrow & Ball, Dulux Trade, Johnstone's, and Little Greene, passing trade discounts directly to you. If you already have your own paint, we are happy to apply labor-only pricing.

How clean do you keep the house while working?

Cleanliness is our top priority. We use dust-extracting sanders, carpet protection film, heavy-duty drop cloths, and vacuum every evening before handover.

Are you fully insured?

Yes, The Gifted Generation carries £5,000,000 Public Liability Insurance, providing total protection for your property and possessions.
